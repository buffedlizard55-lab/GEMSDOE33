Official rules to observe

The DOE/NLR Official Rules PDF (§3.2–3.6, parsed and reviewed 2026-10-04) says the submission is a single GeoTIFF with one raster layer at 100 m resolution for the entire GeoDAWN study area. It allows up to three submissions per week for feedback but requires the competitor to choose one final submission across both prize phases. A finalist must provide complete code assets/documentation sufficient to reproduce predictions.

Generative-AI disclosure is required in the final submission narrative. The rules require describing the extent of AI use and how it was used to develop all submission elements. Do not confuse this narrative disclosure with the optional short competition note.

Artifact format and local audit

The official submission-format instructions describe a single-layer float32 GeoTIFF on the competition grid (UTM Zone 11N, EPSG:32611, 100 m) with predictions in [0,1]. They describe null/NaN outside the prediction bounds. The repository audit also enforces a conservative local policy of finite values everywhere and zeros in template cells outside the survey footprint. This extra project policy is distinguished from the published format rules.

Owner-observed portal message: “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” while using an older NaN-outside file (IR-PORTAL-01). NaNs are a plausible cause; no organizer ruling confirms the cause. The current artifacts avoid NaNs and pass the local audit, but a local pass does not guarantee portal acceptance.
