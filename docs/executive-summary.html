Under the competition DTI (α=0.2 per false positive, β=0.8 per missed truth, 300 m kernel), every isolated prediction dot costs roughly 1 unit of weighted false-positive mass unless it lies within 300 m of a truth pixel, while dense clusters of dots share the same truth credit. Removing low-credit, catalogue-adjacent dots can therefore raise DTI even though no new fault is found: the most plausible mechanism is FP-mass reduction, not discovery. That is an inference from audited bytes, not proof, and no organizer receipt ties 0.2778 to B2's exact bytes.
