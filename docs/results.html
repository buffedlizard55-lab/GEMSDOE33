2. Mathematical Proof: Why GEMSDOE32 (h33-h33-2-b2-...-zeros, 0.2778) Achieved the Highest Score Among the Focus Sites

  1. Verified Exact Set Identities Across Focus Sites (evidence/focus_sites_forensic_20261025.json):
    • GEMSDOE30 $\equiv$ GEMSDOE25 (D2.8, 44,090 px, identical SHA-256 426073b6b4ab...).
    • GEMSDOE31 $\equiv$ GEMSDOE28 (H27-4) $\equiv \{x \in \text{D2.8} \mid d_{\text{cat}}(x) > 1.0\text{ px}\}$ (40,199 px; removes exactly 3,891 dots at $d_{\text{cat}} = 1\text{ px}$).
    • GEMSDOE32 (H33-2-B2) $\equiv \{x \in \text{D2.8} \mid d_{\text{cat}}(x) > 2.0\text{ px}\} \equiv \{x \in \text{H27-4} \mid d_{\text{cat}}(x) > 2.0\text{ px}\}$ (37,654 px; removes an additional 2,545 dots in $1 < d_{\text{cat}} \le 2.0\text{ px}$, for 6,436 total halo dots removed).
    • GEMSDOE36 (38,854 px, 0.2756) contains GEMSDOE32 (37,654 px) as an exact subset plus 1,200 extra dots at $d_{\text{cat}} > 2.0\text{ px}$.
  2. Why Pruning the $B=2$ Catalogue Flank ($d_{\text{cat}} \le 200\text{ m}$) Deterministically Increases DTI: In the competition metric $\text{DTI} = \text{TP}_w / (\text{TP}_w + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w + 0.8\,\text{FN}_w)$, known catalogue pixels $\mathcal{C}$ are masked out of the test set, and $\text{FP}_w$ is summed over emitted dots while $\text{TP}_w$ integrates the 300 m (`3.0 px`) triangular kernel $K_{300}(\hat{Y})$. Because upstream H19-5 was trained on 1-pixel dilated catalogue traces with $\sigma \in \{1,2,4,8\}\text{ px}$ smoothed geophysical gradients, its ridge crest develops a wide side-lobe halo at $100\text{ m}-200\text{ m}$ ($1\text{ px}-2\text{ px}$) alongside known training faults. Those 6,436 halo dots (`3,891` at $d_{\text{cat}}=1\text{ px}$ and `2,545` at $1 < d_{\text{cat}} \le 2\text{ px}$) sit on the shoulder of already-masked training faults, contributing $\Delta\text{TP}_w \approx 0$ on unmapped faults while paying full $\alpha=0.2$ false-positive penalty (`14.6%` of the entire `D2.8` dot budget). Meanwhile, the `2,171` dots retained at $2 < d_{\text{cat}} \le 3\text{ px}$ still extend their 300 m triangular kernels all the way to $d_{\text{cat}}=0$ at fault junctions!
  3. Why GEMSDOE36 (0.2756) Scored Lower Than GEMSDOE32 (0.2778): At $\text{DTI} \approx 0.2778$, a new dot must achieve marginal recall $\Delta\text{TP}_w / \Delta\text{FP}_w > \alpha\,\text{DTI}/(1-\beta\,\text{DTI}) = 0.0714$. Appending `1,200` lower-precision dots onto GEMSDOE32 inflated $\text{FP}_w$ by `+3.19%` without meeting that threshold, reducing DTI by `-0.0022`.
  4. Can a Submission Exceed 0.2778 (and Reach 0.3195 / 0.4413)? Post-hoc pruning of H19-5 / D2.8 is exhausted near `~0.28` and any further subset of D2.8 violates the Parallel-Run Protocol duplicate guard (`>70%` dots within 3 px). Exceeding `0.2778` and approaching `0.3195` (`~+20%` $\text{TP}_w$ at fixed `37,654`-dot budget) or `0.4413` requires genuinely independent fault discovery on concealed intrabasin and step-over structures via (a) domain-invariant multi-physics standardization (`H33-R3-1`) and (b) field-verified fine-scale fault supervision (`DOE GDR #1682 BRIDGE GIS`, currently blocked in this sandbox).
