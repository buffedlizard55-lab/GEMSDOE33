Forensic answer for the reported 0.2778

Measured bytes: GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 are identical. GEMSDOE31 solo is an exact subset of that 44,090-dot d2.8 raster after removing 3,891 dots exactly 1 pixel from the mapped catalogue. GEMSDOE32 B2 is an exact subset of solo after removing 2,545 dots at distances greater than 1 and at most 2 pixels. GEMSDOE36 Anderson contains every B2 dot plus 1,200. B2/Anderson binary Spearman is 0.9843; solo/B2 is 0.9676.

Scientific interpretation: the inherited lineament field may carry useful fault geometry, while 2.8-pixel spacing reduces redundancy and B2 catalogue exclusion removes predictions likely to pay the DTI false-positive term without covering omitted truth. The reported score ordering is consistent with denominator thrift. It is not causal proof: artifacts are dependent, catalogue proximity is not hidden truth, owner reports are unauthenticated, and contacts/basin edges can mimic faults.

The exact B2 family itself fails this project's duplicate lane: correlations above 0.90 and near-total 3-pixel nesting are common. A new file cannot be justified by making B3 or another strict subset in the analog lane.
