3 · H33-H
Geodetic dilatation-rate edges × magnetic horizontal-gradient ridges, strike-discordant from the catalogue
NOT RUN.
  • geod_dilaterate (band 8) gradient / zero-contour neighbourhood
  • tmi_hg (band 3)
  • catalogue strike (local structure-tensor or nearest-trace azimuth) as a discordance gate
Active Basin-and-Range strain localizes on normal faults. Dilatation-rate edges mark hanging-wall/footwall transitions. Magnetic HG ridges whose azimuth differs from nearby mapped traces may be unmapped or newly activated structures. The public catalogue is a geologic surface-trace inventory, not a geodetic strain map. Discordant active-strain magnetic edges can exist without a compiled Quaternary trace. H19-5 used geodetic bands as generic ensemble features. This hypothesis is a dilatation-edge × mag-HG conjunction with an explicit strike-discordance gate against the catalogue, which was not C2 (tip-to-tip relays) and not H33-F. moderate, coverage-dependent

Uphill-facing scarps can mark unrecognized faults, landslide headscarps, or lake shorelines. 5 · H33-I
Volcanic-vent alignment corridors as hidden-fault priors

Basaltic vents in the Basin and Range often exploit hidden faults. Alignments of ≥3 vents define a corridor; magnetic/gravity ridges inside a narrow buffer and off-catalogue are the candidate traces. Vent locations are magmatic, not a Quaternary fault compilation. A vent-aligned magnetic ridge can mark a structure that never ruptured the surface in the mapped interval. No reviewed GEMSDOE emission used the 21-vent CSV as an alignment prior.
