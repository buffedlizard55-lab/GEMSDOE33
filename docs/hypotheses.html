Rank 1 — H33-C2 stepover relay-bridge faults IMPLEMENTED · CORRECTED P1 FAIL · NOT SLOT-CLEARED

  • Layers: INGENIOUS Quaternary-fault vectors from a pinned community mirror of the GDR #1391 compilation (NAD83; hashes pin mirror bytes, not official accuracy). GDR #1391 lists a newer Quaternary Faults v2 that supersedes v1; the mirror's exact version correspondence is unverified. Training band 18, iso_grav_anom_hg; h27-4 owner-reported 0.2708 baseline.
  • Physical signature: approach of tips belonging to distinct feature IDs (300–2,500 m separation, ≤30° strike difference), bridged by sparse dots about 283 m apart only where band 18 is above the local in-footprint 80th percentile within 200 m.
  • Why it may find an omitted fault: the bridge represents a structural connector/relay hypothesis; its existence and omission from the target truth are not independently confirmed. The 2015 regional Great Basin structural-settings paper provides context; it is not the NBMG OF 03-27 map source (OSTI record 1724082).
  • Difference from prior work: GEMSDOE27 T-v2 filled same-polyline compiler gaps and failed a live result; C2 restricts to distinct feature IDs and adds a gravity-edge gate. Its first P1 run leaked full catalogue labels and Qfaults source systems into the test.
  • Corrected conditional diagnostic: the full-data artifact has 940 added dots (41,139 total), but fold-specific base rebuilding and Qfaults source-ID exclusion give P1 mean ΔDTI −0.0007220038 (0/4 folds positive). Across folds, 2,643 added dots earn zero incremental TPw and add 2,643 FPw. P2 remains +0.0002831018. Numeric gate FAIL; no slot. The fixed H19-5 ridge is not re-derived per fold, so the result is conditional.

Rank 2 — H33-A analog-field transfer with a domain-adaptation bound DESIGN ONLY · UNVALIDATED

  • Potential layers: Dixie Valley fault-map files listed by GDR #207; the Desert Peak–Brady preliminary map is described on the official NBMG OF 03-27 product page (free PDF ZIP and geospatial PDF listed; no GIS vector archive confirmed there); GDR #1288 geothermal-feature/classification layers. The analog data are not yet aligned or audited in this repo; the GDR #207 archive request failed TLS/connection setup and no bytes were obtained, so that path is not currently runnable.
  • Feature compatibility: geolocate the actual source data, check CRS/resolution/coverage and label semantics, then define only genuinely overlapping, equivalently processed variables. Do not assume that analog sites have the GeoDAWN 19-band training raster. GDR #1288's geothermal features and class labels are not fault labels and are not equivalent to those 19 bands.
  • Bound and test: Ben-David et al. (2010) give εT(h) ≤ εS(h) + ½ dHΔH(DS, DT) + λ, with λ the joint error of the best shared hypothesis. A classifier-two-sample test on the pre-defined common features can estimate a divergence term; it does not bound λ by itself or prove label compatibility. Use site-blocked source holdouts, geospatial/label QC, and target proxies before transferring anything.
  • Why it may add information / difference from prior work: analog maps may resolve structures missing from regional compilations, but that omission class and equivalent labels remain unverified. The supplied campaign ledger does not document a completed transfer experiment; H33-A is a plan, not a result.
  • Expected DTI and cost: unknown until data alignment and validation; potentially high upside but high uncertainty and medium-high integration cost. Public listings were verified; bytes are not ingested.

Rank 3 — H33-B shallow thermal-probe residuals × gravity edges DESIGNED · NOT RUN

  • Layers: GDR #1391 lists a 2 m temperature-probe archive; its bytes are not in the restored candidate cache. Band 18 iso_grav_anom_hg is the candidate edge gate; band 5 is iso_grav_anom_slope (gravity derivative, not thermal). The USGS DOI 10.5066/P9BZPVUC heat-flow/well-residual products would be independent context, not the same product as 2 m probes.
  • Physical signature: residualize shallow probe temperatures against a pre-registered regional and topographic background, then test coherent positive residuals near gravity edges with spatially blocked, matched controls.
  • Why it may find an omitted fault / difference: shallow fluid-flow anomalies could occur without a preserved surface scarp. This is confounded by springs, season, elevation, land cover, and uneven probe density. Unlike discharge-point screens, this tests a residualized spatial field; no result yet.
  • Expected DTI and cost: unknown; medium effort after archive retrieval and sampling/provenance review.

Rank 4 — H33-D radiometric K/Th alteration corridors PARTIALLY PRE-EMPTED · RECONCILE FIRST

  • Layers: GeoDAWN radiometric K/eU/Th grids and gravity context (bands 13/18 as recorded in the training metadata).
  • Physical signature: test whether spatially coherent K/eU and Th/K anomalies align with independent gravity lineaments, with land-surface and survey-edge controls.
  • Why it may find an omitted fault / difference: alteration could mark a fluid pathway without a Quaternary scarp, but this is an indirect and confounded signal. GEMSDOE29 appears to have tried a radiometric arm; its primary record is not retrieved, so novelty and expected improvement are unknown.
  • Cost: low-to-medium only after the old result, bands, and preprocessing are reconciled. Do not rerun or claim novelty before that audit.
