Why pruning might help — inference, not score attribution

The local metric rewards a maximum nearby match, so redundant predictions can add false-positive cost without proportional new recovery. Deleting low-credit prediction mass can help; deleting near a real missing parallel fault can hurt. The exact net change requires held-out truth, which is unavailable for the reported organizer result. Neither the score list nor a source-code projection proves causation or predicts a higher competition score.
