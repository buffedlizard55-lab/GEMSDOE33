1. AT1 — Cover-stratified, field-held-out transfer of magnetic/gravity edge-normal agreement

Layers: Cached TMI, isostatic gravity, basement-depth and LiDAR step/coherence channels; independent field fault geometries from DOE GDR 1682 BRIDGE and/or GDR 207.

Signature: Learn source-field fault versus lithologic-contact discrimination from normal agreement and cross-edge signed contrasts. Condition covariate-shift weighting on common basement-depth/relief support, not field identity.

Missing-fault rationale: Concealed discontinuities can affect density and susceptibility without a mapped scarp. Independent dense analog traces could teach that class; mere agreement does not establish a fault.

Difference from local prior art: H33-F used the same regional catalogue inside named field masks, not independently mapped extra faults or field-held-out contact discrimination. H33-6 used fixed edge consensus and a baseline swap, not independent-label transfer. Do not rerun either failed arm.

Non-fault mimic: Basin-fill/lithologic contact

Qualitative priority / cost: Highest conditional upside in this lane; unknown sign until independent-label confirmation; Medium-high: GIS audit and independent source-label deduplication dominate; CPU model sufficient

2. AT2 — Analog-transfer of structure-conditioned radiometric alteration residuals

Layers: Owner-mirrored GeoDAWN K/Th/total-count channels, TMI and gravity ridges, basement depth; independent BRIDGE/GDR 207 field fault geometries.

Signature: Within matched relief/depth/lithology strata, measure along-strike persistence of radiometric residuals across independently mapped source faults; transfer only inside common feature support.

Missing-fault rationale: Fluid alteration along permeable buried structures may preserve a weak geochemical halo even when no clear scarp is catalogued. Test against regional lithology, not hot-spring proximity alone.

Difference from local prior art: Current radiometric cache contains scalar/transform channels; H33-F did not fit independent fault-conditioned residual distributions or a field-held-out radiometric negative control.

Non-fault mimic: Alluvial provenance or hydrothermal alteration unrelated to an active permeable fault

Qualitative priority / cost: Medium conditional upside, lower confidence than AT1 because surface transport can dominate; Medium-high; source GIS plus stratum definition and negative controls

BLOCKED: radiometric owner-mirror bytes available and pinned, official survey provenance not reauthenticated; independent field labels absent and official payload hosts unavailable here.

3. AT3 — Field-held-out contrastive transfer of buried-fault cross-section motifs

Layers: Cached det_elev curvature, basement depth, conductivity and gravity cross-edge profiles; independent BRIDGE/GDR 207 field picks.

Signature: Compare oriented multi-layer profiles across source faults with matched unfaulted basin margins; align by local edge normal and transfer the shape, not absolute geographic coordinates.

Missing-fault rationale: Subtle flexural/structural contrasts can recur beneath cover without reproducing a regional mapped trace.

Difference from local prior art: H33-F's per-pixel HGB and H33-6's local edge-normal product do not learn profile-shape contrastive motifs using independent field labels. This is not a new generic curvature transform claim.

Non-fault mimic: Shoreline terrace or erosional basin-margin flexure
