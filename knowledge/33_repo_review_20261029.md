### Pass 3 — task fit and remaining hypotheses

The best evidence-based answer to “why did B2 score highest?” is limited to a forensic, not causal, claim: its raster is a thinned subset in the same H19-5 emission family as the other high owner-reported site results; removing 2,545 dots from the H27-4 set excludes catalogue-flank pixels and changes the spatial footprint. That association is consistent with the competition's spatial scoring behavior, but does not prove the organizer's mechanism or imply the local hide-and-recover proxy can predict the private leaderboard. The score/file pairing and score itself remain owner-reported. No proof exists in this repository that a candidate can beat 0.2778 or 0.4413.

The repository already documents several future research directions. The following ranking is a **research slate, not a validated candidate ranking**; no numerical score estimate is attached:

**No top candidate was run on holdout in this review.** Hypotheses 2–4 require unverified external source bytes, and the method prerequisite must first be separately preregistered. The available evidence does not justify choosing an already-failed feature recipe and reusing its holdout. The external sources are named, but their particular required payloads were not verifiably obtainable within this sandbox's host allowlist. Thus none is currently a viable submission candidate. This is a blocker report, not a claim that a future experiment cannot work.
