## 1. Diagnosis of Prior Failures & Shared-Stack Bug Resolved Before Round 2

1. **Shared Feature-Stack Bug Fixed Once in Template (`IR-33-LIDAR-VOID-01`):**
   Line-by-line inspection of `src/gemsdoe33/features.py` against `data/lidar_scarp_features.json` (`0 = no lidar`) revealed that `1,272,913` in-footprint cells (`24.63%`, concentrated in `NE_LidarGapHeavy`) have `lid_valid == 0`. Previously, `features.py` treated `0` as `NaN` and ran nearest-neighbor `distance_transform_edt` fill across the entire 12,729 km² void — overwriting `lid_valid` with positive values (`1..255`) everywhere and fabricating `260,733` phantom Voronoi high-scarp cells (`lid_step_max > 100`) inside the void. Per Protocol Rule 2 (*"If a shared tool is wrong, fix it once in the template and report it; never keep a private fork"*), `src/gemsdoe33/features.py` now preserves exact `0.0` in `lid_valid` and explicit `NaN` in `lid_*` where `0 = no lidar`. All 109 non-LiDAR channels retain identical definitions.
2. **Why `A33-L1` Failed (`0.108374` vs `B1_regional = 0.113244`, `0/4` folds):**
   - Used only 12 channels (omitting 1 m LiDAR scarp morphology where valid, crustal strain/seismicity, magnetic tilt curvature, and contractor radiometric alteration ratios).
   - Warped global marginal CDFs across incompatible cover regimes (forcing steep range-front scarps in Dixie Valley to match flat basin floors, and concealed stepovers in Brady/Desert Peak to match exposed bedrock).
   - Sampled negative examples at `d_vis > 3 px` inside active geothermal stepovers — mislabelling unmapped intra-stepover hydrothermal splays at 300–800 m as non-faults.
   - Emitted via greedy point-priority `d=2.8 px` thinning rather than maximizing expected coverage under the 300 m triangular DTI kernel.

## 3. Frozen `A33-R2-CSHT` Experimental & Gate Contract

1. **Zero Label Leakage:**
   - `gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv` and `gdr_volcanic_vents_in_footprint.csv` are read strictly for `(row, col, temp_c, geothermquartz_c, thermalclass)` — the `dist_known_fault_px` column is **never read** (enforced in code and locked by unit test).
   - Every fold model (`M_A`, `M_B`, `M_C`), negative clearance mask, local-contrast transform, and `d_vis > 2.0 px` exclusion mask consumes **only** `fold["train"]` and `fold["visible"]`.
   - All **34** candidate channels are tested individually by `SegmentHoldout.canary()` before any model fitting; any two-sided fold or pooled `AUC > 0.90` aborts immediately.
2. **Primary Screen (`Experiment 2 / 3`, `split_id = "quadrant-buffered-15px-v1"`):**
   - Exact vendored `vendor/gems24_template/holdout.py::SegmentHoldout(footprint, catalogue, buffer_px=15)`, `evaluator_version = "gems-segment-pooled-v1"`, `60,988` pooled withheld positives.
   - Comparable incumbent best: `B1_regional` (exact fold-safe regional expert from `A33-L1`, `HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.113244`), alongside `B0_raw_analog` and `matched_random` at identical fold-scaled dot budgets (`round(37654 * valid.sum() / footprint.sum())`).
3. **Frozen Independent Confirmation (`Experiment 3 / 3`, `split_id = "diagonal-wedge-buffered-20px-confirm-v1"`):**
   - Frozen **prior to observing screen results** (`frozen_before_screen_results = True`).
   - Partitions `footprint` at 45° to the screen axes into 4 disjoint diagonal wedges (`N_wedge`, `E_wedge`, `S_wedge`, `W_wedge` via signs of `(y - y_med) ± (x - x_med)`), hiding every whole 8-connected fault component touching a wedge or its `20 px` (`2.0 km`) collar, purging `train` by `20 px` around `valid | hidden`, masking `visible` pixel-exactly, and scoring exact pooled DTI + paired 10 km block-bootstrap 95% CI via `vendor/gems24_template/holdout.py` (`spatial_components`, `pooled_components`, `pooled_bootstrap`).
4. **Uniqueness Stop Guards (Pre-Placement Surface AND Final Dots vs All 21 Pinned Rasters):**
   - Checked against `registry/focus_rasters_20261026.json` (`21` pinned rasters: `12` canonical focus rasters + `5` 2026-10-25 focus-site rasters + `4` local `GEMSDOE33` rasters).
   - Stop immediately as duplicate if `|Spearman ρ| > 0.90` on pre-placement surface or final dots, OR if `> 70%` of candidate dots fall within Euclidean `3 px` (`300 m`) of any single registry raster's dots.
5. **Promotion Decision (`scripts/check_promotion.py`):**
   - `verdict = "promote"` (`eligible_for_selector = True`) **if and only if** all 12 automated gates pass (`screen` and `confirmation` both beat `B1_regional` with paired 95% CIs strictly `> 0` and `>= 3/4` fold wins, `34/34` canaries `<= 0.90`, `21/21` surface & final-dot uniqueness checks pass, saved-byte GeoTIFF validator passes `100%`, note `<= 140` chars, budget `<= 3` experiments and `<= 2` hours). Otherwise `verdict = "negative"` with zero post-hoc retuning.
   - Per Protocol Rule 6, `ok_to_submit` in automated tooling indicates selector eligibility separately from spending a live DrivenData weekly slot (which remains a manual owner/selector step within the 3/week competition cap).
