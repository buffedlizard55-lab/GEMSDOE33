## Plausible mechanism — not a score explanation

Removing predictions within one pixel of known catalogue traces could reduce false-positive mass if the competition's unobserved labels are spatially displaced from the catalogue and those nearby predictions do not help the hidden metric. Conversely, the prune can remove predictions that would receive credit near a genuinely omitted but offset fault. The local artifacts and catalogue-proxy tests do not reveal the private-label outcome. Therefore, this is only a possible mechanism, not evidence that H27-4 “won” or that its pruning caused a particular score change.

The source H19-5 upstream generation code is absent from this checkout. Its fold independence cannot be certified, so local P1 comparisons that keep it fixed remain conditional. The initial truth-conditioned reconstruction experiments and score inversions are withdrawn; see `evidence/first_pass_disposition.json` and `registry/irregularities.json`.
