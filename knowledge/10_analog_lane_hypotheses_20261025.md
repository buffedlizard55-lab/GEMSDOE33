# A33 analog-transfer lane: preregistered hypotheses and experiment

**Frozen before candidate implementation:** 2026-10-25 UTC  
**Lane:** transfer a fault signature from named, better-studied geothermal fields (Dixie Valley, Brady Hot Springs, Desert Peak) while measuring and limiting source/target shift.  
**Evidence boundary:** source field masks and common raster features are restored from SHA-256-pinned owner mirrors. They are not organizer-authenticated. The only locally available source labels are the same public USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue; they are **not independent field-verified analog labels**. No hypothesis below is a competition-score projection.

## Prior work that this slate must not repeat

H33-F pooled the three named field masks, trained one HistGradientBoosting classifier on raw GeoDAWN/LiDAR values, and extrapolated regionally. Its conditional catalogue holdout lost to its control (historical mean ΔDTI −0.093072, 0/4 folds), and its spatial-block domain discriminator AUC was 0.917. That AUC was not an empirical `HΔH` estimate, the joint-label term λ was unknown, and transfer was not licensed. H33-6, C2, H33-D, and the high-scoring registry family are separate edge/reallocation/relay/emission methods, not valid analog-transfer successes.

The nine focus rasters were retrieved from pinned GitHub commits before this slate was frozen. Local pixel analysis shows that the reported high-score family is mostly a common H19-5 subtraction/thinning lineage: GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 are exactly identical; GEMSDOE31/32/36 are strongly nested or near-nested. This motivates a detector-class experiment rather than another edit of those dots.

## Frozen A33-L1 experiment (one of at most three)

1. **Shared evaluator:** exact vendored snapshot of GEMSDOE24’s template `SegmentHoldout` and metric at commit `fc2bc6f350bf5c6ce8da5d7a2ab03578ea1259d1`; evaluator version `gems-segment-pooled-v1`. Hide every 8-connected fault component touching a quadrant and its 1.5 km collar; train only outside the buffered fold; derive every catalogue mask from `fold.visible`; score pooled TPw/FPw/FNw with α=0.2, β=0.8 and the 300 m triangular kernel. The raster-component proxy is not an expert trace-ID segmentation.
2. **Leakage canary:** test each of the 12 source channels alone. Any two-sided fold or pooled AUC >0.90 stops the experiment before placement.
3. **Comparable baselines:** (B0) the fold-safe raw pooled analog expert reproducing H33-F’s model class under the shared evaluator; (B1) a fold-safe regional visible-fault expert using the identical channels, training domain, samples, HGB configuration, B=2 visible-catalogue exclusion and dot budget. The promotion comparator is `max(B0, B1)` by pooled HOLDOUT-DTI; no historical score from another evaluator is called comparable.
4. **Candidate:** A33-L1 uses deterministic 257-knot source-to-target quantile maps, equal source-field contribution, the same fixed HGB configuration, and rank fusion `0.80 * regional_rank + 0.20 * adapted_analog_rank`. No labels from a held-out component or validation quadrant may enter fitting, CDF construction, masking, or thresholding.
5. **Emission:** deterministic priority Poisson placement, 37,654 full-footprint-equivalent dots, minimum spacing 2.8 px, and distance >2 px from the fold-visible catalogue. Candidate and baselines use identical budgets/operators. Include a matched-N uniform eligible control for context.
6. **Promotion gate:** candidate pooled HOLDOUT-DTI must exceed both baselines; paired 95% spatial-block-bootstrap ΔDTI interval versus the best baseline must be strictly above zero; candidate must win at least 3/4 folds; all canaries and both uniqueness stages must pass. Failure is a negative deliverable and forbids slot promotion.
7. **Uniqueness:** before placement compare the continuous full-data A33-L1 surface against every cached focus-registry raster; after placement compare final dots. Stop if any Spearman ρ >0.90 or if >70% of candidate dots lie within 3 px of one registry raster.
8. **Output:** if uniqueness passes, write a new zero-outside and NaN-outside GeoTIFF through the shared strict writer even when the scientific verdict is negative, clearly marked research-only. The zero-outside file is the portal-range-safe format candidate. A valid format does not authorize a weekly slot or prove portal acceptance.
