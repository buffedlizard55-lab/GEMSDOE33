**Frozen before the H33-6 implementation and holdout run.** This is a research-priority slate, not a preregistration of competition truth. H33-6, H33-7 and H33-8 are the three new concepts; H33-9 is explicitly a carried-forward design and H33-10 is a related, partly pre-empted variant. `expected DTI` is ranked qualitatively; no numeric score gain is forecast. ## Frozen H33-6 diagnostic protocol

1. **Comparator:** reconstruct the H27-4/H19-5 `d=2.8` control using the fold-visible catalogue only. The full-data reconstruction is required to match the pinned 40,199-pixel control exactly before any fold result is accepted.
2. **Candidate:** derive an edge-consensus score from the fixed layers above using a 2-pixel Gaussian scale, robust within-footprint standardization, absolute dot-product alignment of magnetic/gravity edge normals, and the strongest basement/conductivity edge as corroboration. Do not use held-out catalogue geometry to compute the score or select pixels.
3. **Emission intervention:** reallocate `floor(0.05 * eligible_control_dots)` of the fold-specific control budget: remove that many lowest consensus-score control dots (score ties by row-major cell index), then add the same number of highest-scoring eligible edge maxima (score ties by row-major cell index), outside the visible catalogue/prune buffer and at least 2.8 pixels from the remaining control and other additions. No threshold, layer, fold, or budget tuning after outcomes are observed.
4. **Holdout:** four spatial folds of 600 m buffered catalogue systems. Score only held-out label pixels with the official 300 m distance-weighted Tversky implementation. This is P1 catalogue-hidden proxy evidence, not private expert truth. P2 is the separate SGMC off-catalogue compilation proxy.
5. **Controls/gate:** compare to the paired fold-rebuilt baseline and a fixed set of 10 matched-random reallocation controls per fold. The numeric gate requires positive mean P1 delta, a strict majority of positive folds, P2 delta no worse than -0.001, and higher mean P1 than the matched-random controls. Report each fold, all emitted counts, and the source/provenance caveats.
6. **Stop rule:** a failure ends H33-6 for this round; no retuning and no slot. Even a numeric pass is conditional until H19-5 is re-derived per fold and a fresh, independent confirmation is run. Any TIFF built from this arm is a unique, format-checked **research artifact only**, not slot-approved and not a contest-score forecast.

## Data access decision

The BRIDGE/Gabbs official pages and metadata establish that data are publicly listed and identify licenses/files; they do **not** establish local availability. Both direct binary download attempts in this sandbox failed with TLS/HTTP 000. Those transfer candidates are therefore blocked, not viable. Use no external archive bytes until retrieved, hashed, unpacked, schema-audited, and overlap-checked.
