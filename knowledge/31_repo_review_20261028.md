### Pass 2 — risks, bugs, and assumptions

- **Scientific conclusion:** A33-R7 PHCONG's apparent high absolute HOLDOUT-DTI under CELF was not sufficient: its same-operator random control was better. PHCONGDT also lost to its dot-thin random control; PCSUP did not establish a positive paired improvement. Exp 1's promote label was withdrawn. Do not infer that high absolute DTI or a unique raster predicts a high competition score.
- **Holdout-density limitation:** the documented 2.5% fold emission versus 0.73% deployment density makes random emission unusually competitive under the 300 m kernel. Re-baseline candidate and control at deployment density before interpreting another campaign. This is a design limitation, not grounds to retune a completed holdout.
- **Protocol completeness:** current docs correctly describe the rule that catalogue-derived inputs must be rebuilt fold-wise. The recent experiments are documented as completed; the report and selector should be treated as evidence records, not as independent authentication of every historical measurement.
- **Submission format:** local saved-byte checks verify the stated grid/range rules for the PCSUP artifact, but format-valid is not organizer acceptance. The earlier portal range error remains unresolved as an organizer-side behavior; the audit cannot establish its cause. No portal upload was made.
- **Provenance:** data are owner-mirror/cache inputs; SHA-256 proves byte identity only, not that a byte came from the official source. DrivenData access is not performed here.
- **Website reproducibility:** build is deterministic in this checkout and tests cover site links/build contracts. No browser-based visual review was available in this pass.

1. **Repair the evaluation operating point first (low–medium cost):** rebuild the existing fold-safe hide-and-recover pipeline and all baselines at deployment density (0.73%), retain whole-segment spatial withholding/buffering, and use an emission-matched random control. This is methodological groundwork rather than a new geological candidate. It uses cached features and official owner-mirror inputs already staged; baseline parity and density are prerequisites for credible ranking.
2. **Fold-safe H19-5 re-derivation (medium cost):** independent reconstruction from fold-visible inputs only. The nine-site submissions appear to share a thinning/pruning family; verify whether that reported leaderboard advantage survives holdout before developing a distinct variant. Do not copy B2 or tune on held-out results.
3. **CURVTRACE (higher cost, externally blocked):** raw high-resolution topographic curvature/orientation-tracked scarps from USGS 3DEP could target low-relief buried/eroded traces. Official source: [USGS 3DEP](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program). The needed raw tiles are not in the pinned local input set and were not verified obtainable from this environment; stage exact tiles, CRS, coverage and licence before considering viability. Terrace risers, roads and drainage benches are named non-fault mimics.
4. **COULOMB stress-transfer (high cost, blocked):** georeferenced fault traces and slip geometry from an official DOE GDR dataset are needed to model Coulomb stress lobes and blind synthetic splays. Official listing: [DOE Geothermal Data Repository #1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391). The required trace geometries/CRS were not established in the staged data; not viable until source bytes and CRS are audited. Intrusive/caldera contacts and uncertain friction/slip assumptions are mimics.
5. **PALEOTEMP / relict hydrothermal indicators (high cost, blocked):** official shallow-temperature probes plus mapped sinter/tufa may reveal dormant conduits beyond active vents. DOE GDR #1391 is the named official source, but the specific payload was not staged or verified obtainable here. Paleochannel heat transport can mimic the signal. Do not describe as viable until coverage, licence, CRS, and leakage-safe data availability are checked.

These are research priorities, not an invitation to exceed the 3-experiment/2-hour budget or alter the current lane. First next session task is the deployment-density baseline audit, followed by a fresh frozen lane and slate only if the audit is sound.
