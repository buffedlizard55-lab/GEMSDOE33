# Withdrawn historical confirmation plan

**Status: withdrawn; not a preregistration, validation result, or submission-promotion gate.** The
plan was drafted after first-pass exploration and used an invalid evaluation instrument. A later
review found that the pseudo-hidden test truth was sampled from known catalogue locations and then
used by `reconstruct_shadow` to add predictions at those locations; the reconstruction radius was
also tuned against owner-reported leaderboard scores. Confirmation seeds and the chosen budget were
reused from exploration. These flaws invalidate the apparent gains regardless of the frozen gate.

The full original plan and its exploratory numbers are preserved at
[`archive/withdrawn_first_pass/knowledge/02_preregistration_confirmation_historical.md`](../archive/withdrawn_first_pass/knowledge/02_preregistration_confirmation_historical.md)
for audit only. Do **not** rerun it or cite its results as evidence. See
[`evidence/first_pass_disposition.json`](../evidence/first_pass_disposition.json) and
[`registry/irregularities.json`](../registry/irregularities.json).

No candidate has passed a valid spatially blocked evaluation against the current best comparable
emission. No candidate is approved for a weekly submission slot. Future candidates must have an
immutable design and thresholds recorded before any measurement and predictions must be generated
without the held-out fault geometry.
